Need to Knows

Nigh Drops are a spontaneous IRL moment — to bring people together at a specific place and time, within 24 hours of going live. Hosts set the time, the experience, number of spots, and how they want to share it — then track who showed up. No algorithm, no endless feed, just intentional IRL presence.
Anyone. Artists, creators, businesses, organizations, friends, and communities. If you've got a moment worth showing up for, Nigh is for you.
Nigh is invite-only while we build a trusted community of people who love showing up in real life. As members join, they can invite others, and access opens up gradually.
Nigh is starting in the United States. We're gradually expanding to more places over time — join now and you'll be among the first when Nigh reaches your area.
Joining Nigh is free. Hosting is free too — for priced Drops there's a 5% + $1 per-ticket processing fee, so hosts only pay when they earn. No credit card required to join.
Yes. Guests view and reserve spots for Drops through the Nigh app. A link shared from a host or friend serves as an invitation and provides access to the app.
Nigh /nī/ means near in place, in time, and in relationship. That's where the best things in life happen.
Privacy-first by design — no third-party ads, and we never sell your data. Your info is used to verify identity and shared only when needed, like when you reserve a spot or follow a host.
