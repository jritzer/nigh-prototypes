Nigh — Face Check-In (prototype)

Optional verification with facial check-in. The guest verifies once in the Nigh app — legal ID (name and date of birth) plus a live face check — and gets the verified badge, which includes Face Check-In. At the door a host team member runs a live face check in the Nigh Host app against that drop’s verified guest list. QR and manual check-in stay as fallbacks. The two phones are linked — check in Maya on the host phone and her ticket updates on the guest phone.
