Nigh — Face Check-In (prototype)

Opt-in facial authentication at check-in. The guest sets it up once in the Nigh app and earns a Face Check-In badge; at the door a host team member scans their face in the Nigh Host app. QR and manual check-in stay as fallbacks. The two phones are linked — check in Maya on the host phone and her ticket updates on the guest phone.
